# Typewriter sound sources

These short, adapted WAV assets are used only inside Ghostwriter's opt-in sound feature.
The full source recordings are not shipped.

- `typewriter-strikes.wav`: eight 220 ms strikes cut from [old typewriter](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-old-typewriter-30090/) by monotraum (Freesound). Cuts begin 40 ms before peaks at 1.56, 2.09, 2.93, 3.37, 4.08, 4.82, 6.32, and 6.89 seconds, with short fades. Played as a sample pool for letter, punctuation, space, and erase.
- `typewriter-return.wav`: 1.5 seconds from [typewriter scrolling](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-typewriter-scrolling-44786/) by keus92 (Freesound), starting at 3.4 seconds, with short fades. Played when Enter/Return creates a new manuscript paragraph in Write or guided Reflect. Shift+Enter, IME confirmation, and Enter in the title do not play this cue.

Both source pages offer downloads under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). Adapted on 2026-10-06, converted to 24 kHz mono PCM WAV. The source tracks should be rechecked before reuse outside this app.
